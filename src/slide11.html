Cost Discipline

Total project cost across 9 days: $74 gross, $62 net.

Net of avoidable cluster runtime: $44 to $51 for legitimately required infrastructure.

The discipline applied:

  • Destroy at the end of every working session, by default
  • Measure avoidable cost separately from unavoidable cost
  • Tag every resource at creation time, not after

Destroy and rebuild costs roughly $1.50 per cycle.

Overnight savings are smaller than the cost of debugging stale state.

Cost discipline is a habit, not a rule.
